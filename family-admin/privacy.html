Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Family Admin is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (compose): to create email drafts in the owner's account for the owner to review. Drafts are only sent by the owner.
  • Google Drive (read only): to read files the owner chooses to attach to those drafts. It cannot change or delete Drive files.

What it stores and shares

The app does not keep copies of emails or files, does not sell or share data, and does not use data for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact the owner through the GitHub profile that hosts this page.
