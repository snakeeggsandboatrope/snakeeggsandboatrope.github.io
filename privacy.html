Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Regs Library is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (send only): to send a weekly summary email from the owner's account to the owner's own address. It cannot read, search or delete email.
  • Google Drive (only files the app created): to save and update public regulation documents and notes in one folder. It cannot see any other Drive files.

What it stores and shares

The app works only with public regulatory documents. It does not collect personal information about anyone, does not sell or share data, and does not use data for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact the owner through the GitHub profile that hosts this page.
